⚙️ Main Features of a Hotel/Lodge Management System
1. Reservation & Booking System
Manage online and offline bookings
Prevent double booking
Real-time room availability

👉 This is the core of the system.

2. Front Desk Management
Check-in / Check-out
Guest details & ID records
Room assignment
3. Room & Inventory Management
Track available/occupied rooms
Manage room types (single, double, VIP)
Control hotel items (bedsheets, food, etc.)
4. Billing & Payments
Generate invoices
Accept multiple payment methods
Track income & expenses
5. Housekeeping Management
Assign cleaning tasks
Track room status (clean/dirty)
Laundry management
6. Reporting & Analytics
Daily/monthly revenue reports
Occupancy rate
Business performance insights

👉 Helps decision-making and growth.

7. Restaurant / POS (Optional)
Food orders (room service or restaurant)
Kitchen billing
Stock management
8. Multi-User & Security
Staff accounts with permissions
Admin control
Activity tracking
💡 Benefits of Using HMS
Saves time and reduces manual work
Improves customer experience
Prevents errors (like overbooking)
Increases profit through better management
Centralizes all operations in one system
🧠 Example Use Case

Imagine you own a lodge:

A customer books online
System updates room availability automatically
At arrival → quick check-in
During stay → food orders tracked
At checkout → invoice generated instantly

Everything happens in one system ✅
